Engine model and benchmark

The idle-air valve starts at 32%. The throttle blip adds 8 percentage points for 0.6 s. The idle loop holds its last valve command at 2% driver throttle and resumes at 1% or below. Opening the lab for the first time shows a single blip.

For a 100 RPM shortfall, P adds percentage points of valve command and I adds percentage points per second. For a 100 RPM/s rise, D subtracts percentage points of valve command. This illustrative model includes air delay, inertia and drag. It omits spark control and individual combustion cycles.

The optimizer starts each event from a warmed engine with the pedal released and A/C off. It scores mean RPM error, worst RPM error, valve movement and time at a valve limit. A stalled trial receives a penalty. The tuning priority changes the weights. Lower scores are better.

Compare A/C strategies holds P, I and D fixed and switches only load feedforward. When enabled, the idle loop requests 16 extra valve percentage points while the compressor is engaged. This compensates for the modeled 10 N·m load after the airflow delay.

Settings are saved in this browser when local storage is available. Reset restores 750 RPM, closed throttle and gains of 20 / 10 / 2.
