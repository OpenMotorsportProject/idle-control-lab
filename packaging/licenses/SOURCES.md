# Native notice sources

The Cargo packages for objc2, dlopen2, and webview2-rs omit their repository-level license documents. The copies here came from the revisions recorded in the packages’ `.cargo_vcs_info.json`. The objc2 document matched all five revisions in the release graph and the webview2-rs license matched both of its revisions. [objc2](https://github.com/madsmtm/objc2/blob/d7d2fa23ceaa5e6096c923b081040e5d81b3b9df/LICENSE.md) · [dlopen2](https://github.com/OpenByteDev/dlopen2/blob/cc80e4a0a90d499b677fdf7743699b4b3a43a989/LICENSE) · [webview2-rs](https://github.com/wravery/webview2-rs/blob/edc2caf886175ccaebe86078c9cfe1ae2a187328/LICENSE)

The WebView2 SDK documents come from the [Microsoft.Web.WebView2 1.0.3800.47 NuGet package](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Microsoft.Web.WebView2/1.0.3800.47). The release notice collector verifies the SHA-256 of the crate's x64 static loader against the loader in that package.
